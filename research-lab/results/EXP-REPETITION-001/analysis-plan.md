# Repetition experiment analysis registration

**Status: design registered; no game result.** The coordinator must admit this experiment and reserve a separate compute budget before a match. Freeze the candidate and baseline SHAs, builds, verified UCI behavior, benchmark, ordered opening histories, controls, discovery/holdout families, clocks, seeds, colors, game counts and a manifest digest before starting. Retain every allocated game, including invalid outcomes. No campaign extension based on intermediate scores.

**Primary outcome.** Test candidate vs pinned baseline head-to-head in color-reversed pairs under equal clocks, one thread, hash and hardware. The endpoint is mean points per game (win = 1, draw = 0.5, loss = 0) minus 50%. Include genuine move-history positions with a feasible repeat and ordinary controls, fixed before looking at candidate behavior. Compute separate estimates on the two history classes. Weight classes equally for the registered aggregate and report their frequencies. Holdout controls protect against weakening normal play. One artificial repeated line cannot establish general strength.

**History and mechanism.** A legal replay from the initial position into the UCI history is required for repetition-aware starts. Keep all PGN moves and replay every resulting game. At the decision point, match board placement, side to move, castling and effective en-passant rights, rule-50 counter and supplied move history across arms. For a paired history ablation, keep the same board/turn/castling/effective-EP and counters and change only whether the path is supplied. Report separately eligibility for an immediate repetition under the rules, selection of a repeating root move among eligible opportunities, a third occurrence, and the Fastchess termination reason. A drawn game alone does not prove repetition. Record eligible move and terminal denominators even if zero.

**Inference.** Report raw W/D/L and percentages, paired score, repetition rates with denominators, candidate/baseline medians and all benchmark measurements. Average opposite colors, then positions within opening family. Average the declared clocks within family before estimating the overall effect. For simultaneous 95% intervals over the registered score comparisons, use a two-sided bounded family-level Hoeffding interval with Bonferroni adjustment. Correlated seeds, clocks and repetitions of one position do not increase the independent family count. Changes in repetition frequency and draw rate are behavioral endpoints. A score interval spanning zero is inconclusive; an interval wholly within the score equivalent of ±3 logistic Elo supports a small effect at that resolution. Any positive local estimate requires representative validation before claiming general Elo. With missing or abnormal games give observed totals and worst-/best-case sample effects; withhold directional conclusions. A speed change is reported separately.
