# Analysis rules for EXP-ADVERSARIAL-001

Freeze the candidate, opponent versions, both clocks, opening splits, allocation and this analysis plan before examining match results. The opening suite is intentionally enriched for unusual positions. The target population for these estimates is the declared suite and opponent panel. Training history and discovery outcomes may guide future work; only the untouched holdout contributes to the primary decisions.

- **Objective screen.** Average the candidate's points (win = 1, draw = 0.5) in both colors against the pinned Stockfish baseline, then equally over declared clocks. Compare with 50%. A local head-to-head result on unusual positions can justify a broader strength test. Official validation and representative openings are required for a general Elo claim.
- **Practical screen.** Against each independent opponent, pair a candidate game with the corresponding baseline game at the same opening, color and clock. Estimate both the change in outright win rate and the change in chess points. Give equal weight to colors within position, positions within a dependence group, groups within the split, and opponent-by-clock cells in the overall estimate. Report raw W/D/L alongside the weighted estimates. A jump in wins can coincide with more losses.
- **Dependence and intervals.** Treat opening families (the corpus `family` field) as the dependence groups. The corpus `group` field labels the tactical/general/quiet sampling strata. Compute every cell contrast and the cross-cell averages *within the same group* first. This retains correlation across colors, test arms, clocks and opponents. Use fixed-sample two-sided Hoeffding intervals with a Bonferroni correction for all reported endpoints, clocks, splits and aggregates. The direct head-to-head score has range width 1. Candidate-minus-baseline contrasts have range width 2. Use the full range of possible effects at the boundaries, even for perfect scores or a sample with zero observed variance. Intervals describe sampling uncertainty under independence across declared groups. A curated suite cannot establish performance on opening families outside the suite.
- **Decision.** Call a positive or negative directional screen only when the simultaneous interval lies strictly above or below zero. A result wholly within ±2 percentage points of practical win rate, or within the score equivalent of ±3 logistic Elo head-to-head, can be reported as small at that resolution. A point estimate of zero alone is inconclusive. These are lab reporting margins. Finish every registered cell and retain failed, censored and interrupted runs. Incomplete or mismatched cells yield no affirmative decision.
- **Ceiling.** Report the baseline win rate and the available observed headroom (100% minus baseline win rate). If the baseline already wins every tested game, the sample has no opportunity to demonstrate more wins. Similarity to engine-vs-human play has not been established. Keep opponent-specific behavioral diagnostics separate from outcomes and avoid converting the cross-opponent win contrast to Elo.
