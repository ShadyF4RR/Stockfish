Admit previously approved match manifests from the fork's default branch into `research/testing-queue`. Use the four-hour scheduler and the enrolled Windows worker. Preserve leases and PGNs on interruption. A scheduled tick, a local fixture and PC enrollment each provide different evidence. Verify the actual match receipt before reporting games or Elo.

Engine promotion and public test submission require separate review and statistical evidence. This scheduler can only dispatch chess tests. Keep credentials out of candidate processes and use only the dedicated worker for this PC queue.
